QuaptureIQ Account and Data Deletion

To request deletion of your QuaptureIQ account and associated data, email:

bandup.ielts.support@gmail.com

Subject:
QuaptureIQ account deletion request

Include the email address used for your QuaptureIQ account. We may verify account ownership before processing the request.

What will be deleted

Where applicable, deletion removes:

  • QuaptureIQ authentication and account data;
  • profile data;
  • uploaded images;
  • saved working sessions;
  • associated usage and entitlement records.

Retention and timing

Data may be retained only where required for security, fraud prevention, legal, or financial-record obligations, and only for the required retention period.

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days.
