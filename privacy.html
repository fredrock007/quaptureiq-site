QuaptureIQ Privacy Policy

This policy explains how QuaptureIQ uses information needed to provide the app and manage accounts.

Information we use

Depending on how you use QuaptureIQ, we may process:

  • the email address associated with your QuaptureIQ account;
  • an authenticated user ID used to identify your account securely;
  • photos and other images that you upload for a requested answer;
  • feedback you send through “Help us improve QuaptureIQ”;
  • account, usage, and entitlement data needed to operate the service.

How information is used

We use this information for app functionality and account management. Uploaded images and related request data may be processed by our backend as needed to provide answers and return the requested result.

Data is transmitted over HTTPS encryption in transit. QuaptureIQ does not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Closed-test product analytics

After the closed-test privacy and Data Safety review is complete, QuaptureIQ may enable limited product-usage analytics. The collected events are: selected mode (Question, Source Analysis, Diagram Labels, or Locate); input-source category (scanner, ordinary photo, or device image); answer requested, succeeded, or failed; answer-next usage where the workflow can identify it; answer duration; and a normalized failure stage.

Product analytics does not collect prompts, answers, academic or source content, image bytes, OCR text, email addresses, authentication user IDs, stable device identifiers, credentials, or raw exception text. A random request-correlation value may associate events from one answer lifecycle, but it is not a stable user identifier and is not included in owner dashboard aggregates.

Analytics is stored in QuaptureIQ server telemetry storage and automatically deleted after 90 days. It is available only through an owner-authenticated aggregate reporting API. The public website contains dashboard code only; it does not contain private metrics or credentials.

Guest access

You may use supported guest features without creating an account. Account-only features require authentication and may be associated with the authenticated user ID and account email.

Your deletion request

You may request deletion of your QuaptureIQ account and associated data by emailing bandup.ielts.support@gmail.com. Please include the email address used for your QuaptureIQ account; we may verify account ownership before processing the request.

See the public deletion instructions: https://fredrock007.github.io/quaptureiq-site/delete-account.html
