A little help for parents

Make practice feel possible.

You don't need to do everything at once. Try one short activity, celebrate the effort, and use the memo to talk through tricky answers. Some collections have worksheets and notes rather than full exam papers, and some do not include memos.

2Open the source page and download a PDF.
3Let your learner attempt it first.
4Check the memo if one is provided.
